## Required development policy

Before any development in this project, read and follow [.codereview.yml](.codereview.yml). Its applicable rules govern design, implementation, refactoring, tests, migrations, configuration, and review. Check the affected rules before declaring work complete, and pass this policy path and requirements to delegated workers. Do not silently relax requirements or treat the policy as review-only.

Also follow the enclosing [workspace instructions](../AGENTS.md) and [workspace engineering skill](../.agents/skills/workspace-principal-engineer/SKILL.md). The explicit-approval requirement for implemented fallbacks remains in force; policy references to fallback mechanisms do not grant blanket approval.
